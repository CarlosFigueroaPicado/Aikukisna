**Subject:** Permission request: *Di Nyoo Testiment eena Bileez Kriol* (bzj) for a non-commercial language-learning app

Dear Permissions team,

I lead **Aikukisna**, a free, offline mobile app that helps people on the Caribbean Coast of Nicaragua
learn Miskito, Spanish, Nicaraguan Creole English (Kriol) and Standard English.

Nicaraguan Kriol has very few written resources. *Di Nyoo Testiment eena Bileez Kriol*, published on
eBible.org under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, is one of the largest Kriol texts we know of. Because the licence
does not allow derivative works, we are writing to ask for permission before using it.

**What we are asking for.** Permission to:

1. Align the Belize Kriol verses with an English translation and use the aligned pairs as reference
   sentences in the app's translator.
2. Use the aligned pairs to train a small on-device language model that suggests translations.

**Conditions we propose.**

- The app is free and non-commercial. We would not sell or redistribute the text.
- We would not publish the text as a Bible or as a separate file, and we would not alter its wording.
  Sentences would appear only as short examples, each with the credit line you specify.
- Sentences would be labelled as *Belize Kriol* and marked as pending review until Nicaraguan speakers
  validate them.
- We would remove the material at any time if you ask us to.

If a different arrangement suits you better, such as limiting the use to model training without
displaying the verses, we are happy to adapt.

Thank you for considering this request.
